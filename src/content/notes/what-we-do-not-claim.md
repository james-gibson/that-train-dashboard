# What we do not claim

- **That the results generalise.** OpenTTD is a closed toy economy with clean arithmetic and no
  politics. A mechanism that fails here may work in a real market, and the reverse.
- **That a single run is evidence.** One seed is an anecdote with a timestamp. The design asks for
  arms, and the review asks for the counter-case.
- **That the simulation is neutral.** The scenario author chooses the map, the demand curve and the
  step size. Those choices are recorded so a reader can disagree with them.
- **That this dashboard is a product.** It is a research record with a home page. It has no accounts,
  no search and no support contract, and it is not for sale.

## What it is good for

Testing whether a mechanism behaves the way its argument says it will, cheaply, before anyone spends
money on the argument. That is a real use and a narrow one, and the narrowness is the point.
