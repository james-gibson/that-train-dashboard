# Station subsidies

**Question.** Does a subsidy buy coverage, or does it buy empty trains?

## Design

Three arms on one map:

1. No subsidy — the station closes when it stops paying.
2. Flat subsidy — a fixed monthly transfer per station.
3. Per-passenger subsidy — paid only for delivered passengers.

The measurement is not profit. It is **service**: passengers waiting, journeys completed, and the
month the subsidy stops changing either.

## Why it is still in design

The per-passenger arm needs a scenario hook that does not exist yet, and building it is a week of
work for a result we may not be able to interpret. The skeptic's note is on the page on purpose: a
study that cannot fail is not a study.
