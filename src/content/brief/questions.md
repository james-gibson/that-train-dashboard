# The questions

1. **Does a freight-rate shock propagate, or does it get absorbed?** If a single lane's rate doubles,
   does the network re-route, absorb the cost, or stall? What does the model do at the boundary?
2. **Do station subsidies buy coverage or buy waste?** A subsidy that keeps an unprofitable station
   alive is either a public good or a line item nobody checked. The simulation can show which
   services survive and which become empty trains.
3. **Does timetable discipline survive contact with demand?** A schedule that works at the modelled
   demand may collapse at twice the volume. We want the point where it stops working, not a
   demonstration that it works.

## The question we are not asking

Whether any of this predicts a real economy. It does not, and
[the limits page](/notes/what-we-do-not-claim) says why in more detail. The model is a mechanism
test, not a forecast.
